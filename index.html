MediLingo Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 15, 2026

MediLingo ("we", "our", or "the app") is an educational medical examination preparation application designed to provide medical students and graduates with educational resources, practice questions, revision materials, mock examinations, and related learning features.

This Privacy Policy explains how MediLingo collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the MediLingo application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use MediLingo, we may collect:

  • Name and email address used to create an account.
  • Account authentication information.
  • Learning progress, quiz results, test attempts, and related educational activity.
  • Payment activation information submitted to activate access to the application.
  • Payment receipt images or files when submitted for account activation.
  • Information related to the device used to access the account, for security and account-protection purposes.
  • Technical information necessary for the operation, security, and reliability of the application.

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and maintain account security.
  • Provide access to MediLingo educational content.
  • Save learning progress and examination results.
  • Process and review account activation requests.
  • Prevent unauthorized account access and abuse.
  • Maintain and improve the application's functionality and reliability.
  • Respond to support requests.

3. Firebase Services

MediLingo uses services provided by Google Firebase to support application functionality. These services may include Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Storage.

Information stored through these services is used to provide authentication, account management, application data storage, learning progress, and payment-activation functionality.

4. Payment Information

MediLingo may require an activation payment for access to certain application features. Payment-related information submitted through the application may include a payment receipt or related information required to verify an activation request.

MediLingo does not request or intentionally store users' bank account passwords, ATM PINs, or banking login credentials.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information collected through the application. Data may be stored using third-party infrastructure providers, including Google Firebase.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that support the operation of MediLingo, such as authentication, database, storage, hosting, analytics, or other technical infrastructure providers, where applicable.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or integrity of MediLingo and its users.

7. Educational Content

MediLingo is an educational application. Its medical questions, explanations, and learning materials are intended for educational and examination preparation purposes and should not be considered a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

8. Children's Privacy

MediLingo is intended for medical students, medical graduates, and other users preparing for medical examinations. The application is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

9. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain account and application information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Users who wish to request deletion of their account or associated personal information may contact us using the support contact provided with the application.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page, and the effective date will be updated accordingly.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or MediLingo's handling of information, please contact us through the support contact provided in the MediLingo application.
