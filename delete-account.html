Account Deletion Request

You can request deletion of your MediLingo account and associated personal data using the options on this page.

How it works: Choose an email option below and send us your account deletion request. Your account will not be deleted simply by opening this page.

Request account deletion

  1. Choose one of the email options below.
  2. Include the email address used to register for MediLingo.
  3. Send the message to our support email address.

Open Gmail Deletion Request Open Default Email App

You can also email us directly at briefingers@gmail.com with the subject MediLingo Account Deletion Request.

What data may be deleted?

Depending on the information associated with your account, deletion may include:

  • Your MediLingo account and authentication profile.
  • Personal profile information associated with your account.
  • Learning progress, quiz results, and related learning records.
  • Payment request records and uploaded payment receipts, where applicable and where retention is not legally required.

Processing your request

We will review your request and may ask for reasonable verification to confirm that you own the account. We will then process eligible account and data deletion requests.

Certain information may need to be retained where required for legal, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes. Any retained information will be limited to the applicable purpose and retention period.

Opening an email draft does not submit your request. You must send the email for us to receive it.

Privacy questions

For account deletion or privacy questions, contact briefingers@gmail.com.
