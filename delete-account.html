Account Deletion Request

MediLingo users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data by following the instructions on this page.

Important: To protect your account, please submit the request from the email address associated with your MediLingo account whenever possible.

How to request account deletion

  1. Click the email link below.
  2. Use the subject line MediLingo Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address you used to register for MediLingo.
  4. We may ask for reasonable verification before processing your request.

Email an Account Deletion Request

What data may be deleted?

Depending on the data associated with your account, deletion may include:

  • Your MediLingo account and authentication profile.
  • Personal profile information associated with your account.
  • Learning progress, quiz results, and related learning records.
  • Payment request records and uploaded payment receipts, where applicable and where retention is not legally required.

Processing and data retention

We will review your request, verify that it is authorized, and process eligible deletions. Some information may need to be retained for legal, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes. Any retained data will be limited to the applicable purpose and retention period.

We will contact you if additional information is required to process your request.
